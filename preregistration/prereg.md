## 0. Locked design parameters

- **Datasets:** **Shapes3D primary**, **dSprites secondary**. (MPI3D optional later confirmation only.)
- **Factors + types (Shapes3D):** floor hue, wall hue, object hue, scale, orientation = **continuous** (R²); shape = **categorical** (normalized accuracy). dSprites supplies **x/y position** (continuous) for the position arm.
- **Headline contrast:** object hue (color-jitter, expected-genuine) vs. x/y position (random-crop, expected-artifact).
- **Augmentation conditions × strengths:** Color (hue/color jitter), Position (crop/translation), Orientation (rotation), Scale (random-resized-crop), + Control-aug (minimal); each at **≥2 strengths** (weak, strong).
- **Probe-capacity ladder:** (1) linear · (2) MLP 1-hidden small · (3) MLP 1-hidden large · (4) MLP 2-hidden. **Monotone capacity; capacity reported as an explicit measure (param count / effective DOF) per rung.** Exact widths fixed at probe-build (not a frozen quantity).
- **Seeds:** **≥10 per cell**, seed controls **both** encoder and probe init; bootstrap over seeds. Cell = `(factor, condition, strength, probe_capacity, seed)`.
- **Probe discipline:** probe-train / val / probe-test split; **probe-train size held FIXED across the ladder**; regularization tuned on val, **never** on test; report probe-test only.

## 1. Generative model / dataset
Factor-controlled synthetic datasets where each image's generative factors are known **by construction**. Shapes3D (6 factors above) primary; dSprites (shape, scale, orientation, x, y) secondary.

## 2. "True factor value"
The ground-truth generative factor value for each image, **defined by the dataset's construction, not estimated**. Each factor is the probe's prediction target.

## 3. Recoverability, encoder gain, probe selectivity (the metric layer)

- **Recoverability `R(F,c)`** — probe-**test** performance predicting factor F from the **frozen encoder** at capacity c, on a **normalized [0,1]** scale: categorical → `(acc−chance)/(1−chance)`; continuous → `R²`. **R²<0 retained, not clipped** (clipping biases G upward). ⚠️ orientation is cyclic → circular target / sin–cos at probe-build.
- **Probe selectivity `S(F,c) = R(real labels) − R(random labels)`** — Hewitt–Liang **random-LABEL** control task. Bounds the *probe* (S≈0 ⇒ probe is memorizing).
- **Encoder gain `G(F,c) = R(trained encoder) − R(random ENCODER)`** — matched-architecture untrained baseline. Bounds *representation-free* structure. **G is the headline metric.**
- **Commensurability (FIX 1):** normalized-accuracy and R² are different units. **G is interpreted per-factor** against that factor's own ε_G; cross-factor comparisons restricted to **within-type** factor pairs — R² and normalized-accuracy are never pooled or rank-compared.
- **Capacity gap `Δ_G(F) = G(F, top rung) − G(F, linear)`.**

## 4. "Invariance" (ε-invariance) — the dual-gate

- **Invariance verdict boolean (also the flip-count boolean):** trained encoder is **ε-invariant to F at capacity c iff `G(F,c) ≤ ε_G`**.
- **Genuine recovery requires BOTH gates:** `G(F,c) > ε_G` **AND** `S(F,c) > 0`. Three exhaustive cases (FIX 3):
  - `G ≤ ε_G` → **invariant**; the sub-case `G < 0` ("below-random / actively suppressed") is reported **distinctly**, not merged with "indistinguishable from random."
  - `G > ε_G, S > 0` → **genuine recovery** (a *linear-invariance artifact* when it appears only above the linear rung).
  - `G > ε_G, S ≤ 0` → **inconclusive / probe-driven** (dead zone): not genuine recovery; flagged separately. The invariance boolean keys on G alone, so the flip-count is well-defined.
- **ε_G primary = data-derived per-factor:** upper bound of the bootstrap 95% CI of G under the **random-vs-random-encoder null** (captures **encoder-init noise only**). Capacity-dependent probe over-fitting is controlled by **S**; **both gates use the same paired seed bootstrap**; the AND-gate is a conjunction of two one-sided tests, each component reported. **Fixed ε_G = 0.05 normalized = sensitivity analysis only** (FIX 2, 5).

## 5. Controls
- **Random-LABEL control task** → defines S (bounds probe).
- **Random-ENCODER control** (matched arch, untrained) → defines G (bounds representation-free structure).
- **Supervised-reference encoder** → recoverability ceiling; **diagnostic-only** (not in any decision rule).
- **Encoder-quality gate** → downstream linear-probe accuracy proving the encoder is well-trained **before** any invariance claim.

## 6. Hypotheses (LOCKED) + confirm/refute rules

All tests on **encoder gain G** with **probe selectivity S as the recovery gate**, **≥10 seeds**, bootstrap CIs, **Wilcoxon signed-rank** for paired capacity/factor comparisons, effect sizes with CIs, **Holm** correction across factor×condition cells. Probe-test only.

- **H1 (capacity dependence).** G(F,·) is non-decreasing in capacity and rises materially above linear for ≥1 factor.
  - **Confirm:** capacity gap `Δ_G(F)` bootstrap CI **> 0 and > ε_G** for ≥1 factor (with S>0 at the top rung). **Refute:** all `Δ_G` CIs include 0 or stay ≤ ε_G.
- **H2 (heterogeneity).** The capacity effect differs across factors.
  - **Confirm:** **paired per-seed difference** of `Δ_G` for ≥1 **within-type** factor pair has a Wilcoxon-significant / bootstrap-CI-excludes-0 difference (Holm across pairs). **Refute:** all within-type difference CIs include 0. *(FIX 4 — replaces the earlier non-overlapping-CI test.)*
- **H3 (genuine invariance via control).** Some factors stay invariant at every capacity.
  - **Confirm (existence):** ≥1 factor has `G(F,c) ≤ ε_G` at **every** rung (selectivity CI within ±ε_G across the ladder). **Refute:** no factor stays ε_G-invariant across all capacities.
- **H4 (encoder-vs-projector localization — a TEST of Cosentino et al. 2022, not a discovery).** Invariance concentrates in the projector.
  - **Confirm:** paired **`G(encoder) − G(projector) > 0`** for targeted factors (Wilcoxon, Holm), **widening with augmentation strength**. **Refute:** difference CI includes 0 / negative.

**Headline number — verdict-stability flip count:** # of (factor, condition) cells where the boolean `G ≤ ε_G` changes between the linear probe and the top rung; reported for **both** primary per-factor ε_G and the 0.05 sensitivity ε_G.

## 7. Analysis plan (LOCKED)
- Bootstrap CIs on G, S, and Δ_G across seeds; **paired seed resampling** shared by the G and S gates.
- Effect sizes with CIs for the capacity gap (not just p-values).
- Wilcoxon signed-rank across seeds for all paired comparisons (H1 capacity, H2 within-type differences, H4 encoder−projector).
- **Holm** correction across the factor×condition cell family; state the family explicitly.
- Report **per-cell**; never collapse to a single average that hides heterogeneity (the heterogeneity is the finding).
- **Power caveat (pre-registered):** Wilcoxon at n=10 has limited power for small effects after Holm; a pilot power check may raise seeds before the full sweep.
- **AI-assistance disclosure:** literature search and drafting used AI tools; recorded for transparency.

## 8. Deferred to later phases (NOT frozen quantities)
Exact probe widths (probe-build); the capacity measure plotted against G (param count vs. effective DOF — pre-register at probe-build, engaging Lee & Kondor 2026); orientation circular-regression handling; real-data sanity-check dataset.

### A5 — 2026-07-28 — Shapes3D Orientation arm excluded from the confirmatory grid (augmentation–factor construct mismatch)

**Unblinding status at the time of this amendment:** unchanged from A2–A4 — the H1–H4 statistics layer, flip counts, ε_G, and figures have **not** been run on any real cell, and no targeted-factor verdict has been inspected. This amendment rests **solely on the §5 encoder-quality gate**, computed from the augmentation-**preserved** factor (object **shape**) and by construction orthogonal to every targeted-factor hypothesis (H1–H4 concern the *targeted* factors, never shape). No orientation-recoverability result was examined in drafting it.

**Issue — the Orientation augmentation does not manipulate the Orientation factor.** The Shapes3D orientation factor is generated by **camera azimuth**: a viewpoint change (on SO(3)) that reveals *different faces* of the object. The Orientation-arm augmentation is **2D in-plane image rotation** (SO(2) acting on the projected image and its floor/wall backdrop, object face unchanged). These are distinct group actions, and invariance to one does not entail invariance to the other, so the arm trains invariance to a transform that is **not** the generative factor it is subsequently probed on; a low orientation-recoverability reading from this arm would be uninterpretable as azimuth invariance.

**Corroborating evidence (gate only).** Image-space rotation wide enough to span the factor's ±30° support drives the preserved shape anchor **below the untrained random-encoder floor** (≈0.83), i.e. the encoder fails the §5 trustworthiness criterion: linear-rung shape recoverability = **0.65–0.77 at ±180°** (full 12-seed sweep, 0/12 gate pass; `results/probes/orientation_strong/meta.json`) and **0.80–0.85 at ±30°** (bounded to the exact factor range; subsampled linear-rung check, 3 seeds, with training diagnostics homogeneous across all 12 seeds). No image-rotation setting resolves this: any arc wide enough to cover the ±30° factor destroys shape, while a narrower arc under-manipulates the factor. The failure is **structural (a construct mismatch), not a tunable hyperparameter**.

**Change.** The **Shapes3D Orientation arm (`orientation_strong`) is removed from the realized confirmatory grid** — excluded from the H1, H2, and H3 families and from the headline verdict-stability flip count — and reported as a **labeled methodological limitation** (the gate failure and its construct-validity cause), not as a recoverability row (no gate-passing encoder exists to source one). This narrows the realized grid (A1) from five strong cells to four — **Color, Position, Scale, and Control-aug**.

**Scope — what is and isn't touched.** (1) The exclusion is **arm-level, not readout-level**: it removes the `orientation_strong` *cell*. This is distinct from A3, which removed the `(dsprites, orientation)` *readout column* everywhere — here the orientation **factor remains a valid confirmatory readout in the other Shapes3D cells** (Color, Scale, Control), where the encoder was not trained with rotation augmentation. (2) **No decision rule changes** — every surviving G/ε_G verdict, selectivity, flip count, and test is computed identically; only the realized-grid membership narrows (stated explicitly per §6). (3) Removing the arm drops its targeted comparisons from the H2 within-type and flip families; this **reduces** the multiple-comparison burden on the surviving factors and is disclosed as a direct consequence of removing an untrustworthy arm, **not** a post-hoc power adjustment. (4) H4 is affected only in that orientation is no longer an H4-targeted factor (its `TARGETED_FACTORS` entry is inert with the cell absent); H4's "partial/descriptive" status (A1) is otherwise unchanged. **Implementation:** `orientation_strong` is removed from `hypotheses.EXPECTED_CELLS`; the §5 gate already excludes the arm's 0/12 encoders downstream, so this makes the exclusion principled and disclosed rather than incidental.

**Consistency.** This applies the logic of A1 (principled, pre-data scope curtailment of the realized grid) and A3 (exclusion of a construct-problematic orientation readout) to the Shapes3D case. Orientation has now proven un-manipulable-without-confound on **both** datasets — itself a reported finding on the correspondence between image-space augmentations and 3D generative factors.

Scale's anchor deficit **does not close with probe capacity**: at the top rung the trained encoder is 0.377 *below an untrained* one. The shape information is destroyed, not made nonlinearly inaccessible. Orientation's deficit largely closes (0.677 → 0.928), which is a linear-readout collapse. This distinction is the study's own absent-versus-inaccessible axis appearing inside its trustworthiness gate, and it is why the two exclusions are reported as **mechanistically distinct** rather than as a single attrition statistic. Per A8(b), this three-valued reading (`PASS` / `FAIL-DESTROYED` / `FAIL-INDETERMINATE`) replaces the frozen two-valued gate; Scale is excluded under **FAIL-DESTROYED**, which A8(b) licenses.

**(b) §0 augmentation-divergence disclosure (independent of the gate outcome).** Frozen §0 specifies the Scale condition as "**random-resized-crop**". The implementation actually trained is `T.RandomAffine(degrees=0, scale=(0.75, 1.25), fill=0)` (`src/encoders/augmentations.py`), i.e. a whole-**scene** affine zoom with black border fill, not a crop-and-resize. What failed the gate is therefore the Scale arm **as implemented**; the preregistered random-resized-crop variant was never trained and **remains untested**. This divergence was discovered while diagnosing the gate result and is disclosed here unconditionally. It is **not** grounds to re-run the arm: A6(d)'s rule was pre-committed on the *gate outcome*, and re-training a different augmentation family after a failing result, then admitting it only if it passes, is the researcher degree of freedom that rule exists to remove. Any future random-resized-crop Scale run is **exploratory**, declared in advance, excluded from every Holm family and from the flip count, and reported whatever its outcome.

**(c) Correction to A6(d)'s stated construct rationale.** A6(d) asserted in advance that bounded scale was construct-**valid** ("zoom ≈ apparent size; no 2D/3D mismatch like orientation"). That assertion is **withdrawn as unsupported**, and no replacement mechanism is asserted as established. First, whole-scene affine zoom is not the Shapes3D `scale` factor, which resizes **only the object** inside a fixed, fully-filled rendered scene; the augmentation additionally introduces out-of-distribution black borders at zoom < 1. Second, the natural "black fill destroys the anchor" mechanism is **falsified by this study's own control arm**: Control-aug uses `RandomResizedCrop(64, scale=(0.8, 1.0))`, which introduces no fill and no borders, and its anchor still sits at 0.810, below the 0.829 floor. A competing account consistent with all four Shapes3D arms is a **colour shortcut**: Shapes3D carries 10×10×10 hue combinations, so the (floor, wall, object) hue triple identifies an image 1-in-1000 and the NT-Xent objective is solvable without encoding shape; Color is the only Shapes3D arm that jitters hue and the only Shapes3D arm that passes, with the anchor ordering Color 1.000 ≫ Control 0.810 > Orientation 0.677 > Scale 0.483. Final NT-Xent is 4.949–4.965 with alignment ≈1e-4 and uniformity ≈−3.88 in **every** arm, so no arm is under-trained and the objective is solved everywhere. Under this account both A5's and A7's gate failures could be artifacts of a single-augmentation regime lacking photometric augmentation on a colour-dominated dataset, rather than group-action mismatches. **This is recorded as an open, testable alternative, not as a finding.** The discriminating test is a declared-exploratory **recipe control** (the same scale augmentation composed with the canonical `ColorJitter` + `RandomGrayscale` base); it enters no confirmatory family, and its result is reported either way. Until it runs, the paper states the construct-mismatch and colour-shortcut accounts as **competing** explanations and asserts neither.

**(d) Ratchet stop (new commitment, binding).** The realized confirmatory grid is **frozen at three cells (Color, Position, Control-aug)**. No further cell may be removed from the confirmatory families for any reason, gate-based or otherwise. Should a fourth exclusion ever appear warranted, the study is **re-scoped and reported as a negative result about augmentation–factor correspondence** rather than continuing as a probe-capacity study on a shrinking grid. This converts the A1/A3/A5/A7 curtailment sequence from an open-ended rule into a bounded one, and it is stated before any verdict exists.

**Scope — what is and isn't touched.** (1) **No decision rule changes here** — every surviving G/ε_G verdict, selectivity, flip count, and test is computed identically; only realized-grid membership narrows (stated explicitly per §6). Decision-rule changes are confined to A8. (2) Removing the arm drops its targeted comparisons from the H2 within-type and flip families; per A6(c) the **maximal** pre-registered family (`DESIGNED_STRONG_CELLS`) and every pre-verdict exclusion with its reason are emitted alongside the realized family, so family shrinkage cannot read as power-gaming. (3) **Newly disclosed limitation, stated plainly:** with Scale and Orientation excluded, the two surviving *treatment* arms are Color (Shapes3D) and Position (dSprites), which sit on **different datasets**. A4 §d(1) disclosed this confound for the §0 headline contrast; it now applies to **every** across-arm comparison in the realized grid. The paper therefore makes **no claim of the form "the capacity effect depends on which factor the augmentation targets"**; H2 is reported as a **within-cell, targeted-versus-untargeted** contrast (each surviving cell supplies its own within-type pairs, per A6(d)), never as across-arm factor heterogeneity. (4) A separate limitation, disclosed: the realized grid contains **no dSprites baseline** (Control-aug exists only on Shapes3D), so the Position arm has no matched no-treatment encoder on its own dataset. **Implementation:** `scale_strong` is removed from `hypotheses.EXPECTED_CELLS` and added to `PRE_VERDICT_EXCLUSIONS` with this reason.

**Consistency.** This executes a rule pre-committed in A6(d) on the branch that rule specified, on the same gate-orthogonal, pre-verdict basis as A1/A3/A5, and adds a termination condition (d) that the earlier curtailments lacked.

### A8 — 2026-08-07 — Metric-layer repairs (ε_G, the random-label control, null-headroom, H4's floor) and the A6(b) capacity-axis discharge

**Unblinding status at the time of this amendment:** unchanged from A2–A7 — the H1–H4 statistics layer, ε_G, flip counts, verdicts, and figures have **not** been run on any real cell, and **no targeted-factor recoverability value has been inspected**. Every change below rests on (i) analysis of the estimator code itself, (ii) simulation on synthetic draws, (iii) the random-**encoder** floor levels disclosed in A4 (D015/D019), and (iv) the shape anchor. This amendment is filed **because** the confirmatory layer has never run: these are defects that can only be corrected honestly before a verdict exists, and this is the last moment at which that is true.

**(a) ε_G is redefined as the null *quantile*, not a confidence interval of the null *mean*. (Decision-rule change.)** Frozen §4 specifies ε_G as the "upper bound of the bootstrap 95% CI of G under the random-vs-random-encoder null," and `instrument.epsilon_g` implements exactly that: it pools all ordered seed pairs `R_i − R_j` **and** `R_j − R_i`, then bootstraps the **mean** of that pool. The pool is antisymmetric, so its mean is **identically zero for any data whatsoever**, and the bootstrap therefore returns the standard error of an estimator of zero rather than the spread of the null gain. Quantified on synthetic draws at per-seed σ = 0.05 with S = 12: ε_G = **0.0126**, where the true 97.5th percentile of the null gain distribution is **0.1329** (a 10.5× understatement) and the sampling SE of the paired G being thresholded is **0.0204** — the threshold is *smaller than the noise in the statistic it gates*. It is also **a function of the control sample size**, shrinking as 1/√(S(S−1)): 0.0126 at S = 12, 0.0055 at S = 24, 0.0030 at S = 48. Adding random-encoder seeds would therefore make readouts *more* likely to be called "recovered", which inverts the intended direction and makes the headline unreplicable at a different seed budget. The defect is in the **frozen definition**, not in its implementation, so it is corrected here rather than silently. **New primary:** `ε_G(F,c) = the (1 − α/2) empirical quantile of the random-vs-random null gain distribution` (α = 0.05), which is what §4's own gloss — "how large a gain init noise alone can produce" — describes. **Demoted to co-reported sensitivity, not removed:** the frozen CI-of-the-mean reading, and the fixed ε_G = 0.05 already specified in §4. All three are reported side by side for every readout, and the primary is named in every table.

**(b) The random-label control task (S) is repaired, and the §5 gate becomes three-valued.** *Control task.* `instrument.permute_labels` shuffles each split's label rows **independently**, so the control label is not a function of the image and the control task is unlearnable at probe-test **by construction**; consequently `R(random labels) ≈ chance`, `S ≈ R(real)` always, `S > 0` is satisfied whenever a factor is recoverable at all, and §4's third case (`G > ε_G, S ≤ 0`, the probe-driven dead zone) is unreachable. That is not the Hewitt–Liang control task §3/§9 cite, whose type→label map is held **fixed across splits** precisely so a high-capacity probe *can* fit it. **New definition:** the control task is a random permutation of each factor's **value→target map**, drawn once per (factor, seed) and applied **identically to probe-train, probe-val, and probe-test**. Every factor in both datasets is discrete-valued, so this is well-defined. Disclosed consequence, stated rather than hidden: for a **categorical** factor a value permutation is a pure relabeling, so `S ≡ 0` identically and the dual gate degenerates to a single gate on categorical readouts. This is harmless for the study as realized, because **every targeted factor is continuous** (hue, position, scale, orientation) and shape — the only categorical factor — is the gate anchor, never a targeted readout. For continuous factors the permutation destroys the target's ordinal/metric structure, so S measures exactly what §3 intends. *Gate.* The §5 gate's **PASS** direction is unchanged and remains sound: linear decodability of shape at ≥ 0.90 is sufficient evidence of a non-collapsed encoder, and presence needs no capacity argument. Its **FAIL** direction, however, commits the inference this study exists to indict — non-decodability at the linear rung is precisely the ambiguous evidence the capacity ladder is built to disambiguate. The gate is therefore **three-valued** from here: `PASS` unchanged; `FAIL-DESTROYED` when the trained-minus-floor anchor deficit is negative at **every** rung and does not close with capacity (this licenses exclusion); `FAIL-INDETERMINATE` otherwise (a linear-readout collapse, which does **not** license exclusion on gate grounds alone). Applied retrospectively: Scale is `FAIL-DESTROYED`, so A7's exclusion is gate-licensed. Orientation is `FAIL-INDETERMINATE`, so **A5's "Corroborating evidence (gate only)" paragraph is retro-labeled non-load-bearing**; A5's exclusion stands on its analytic SO(2)-versus-SO(3) construct argument, which requires no measurement, and the reader is told so explicitly.

**(c) Null-headroom: a co-primary headroom-normalized gain, and a calibrated instrument.** A4 §a defined null-saturation and A6 §a promoted it to a gate on H3 and the flip count. The disclosed A4 random-**encoder** floors make the consequence concrete: at the **linear** rung on Shapes3D the floors are floor_hue 0.9890, wall_hue 0.9892, object_hue 0.9275, scale 0.9122, shape 0.9082, orientation 0.9804, so the maximum attainable G is between **+0.011 and +0.092** and shrinks further at higher rungs. Under A6 §a's rule (floor ≥ 0.90 at the linear **or** top rung) **all six Shapes3D readouts are saturated at the linear rung**, including `object_hue`, the left-hand side of the §0 headline contrast, so the saturation-gated headline family is empty on Shapes3D and the flip count would report 0 over **zero eligible readouts**. A null produced by the instrument is not a finding. Three additive changes, no relaxation of A6 §a: (1) **co-primary headroom-normalized gain** `G̃(F,c) = G(F,c) / (1 − R̄_random(F,c))`, a per-readout monotone rescaling of G that expresses gain as a fraction of the headroom actually available, reported alongside raw G everywhere and with the same paired-seed bootstrap; (2) **an empty saturation-gated family raises rather than silently reporting zero** — the count is emitted as `undefined` with the eligible-readout set printed; (3) **instrument headroom calibration**, run **blind** on the shape anchor and the A4 §b raw-pixel reference ladder only (never on a targeted factor): the probe-train size and a **held-out-factor-value (extrapolation) probe split** are swept, and the configuration is pinned to the one where the random-encoder floor stays below the A4 saturation level at the top rung on both datasets. The §0 discipline that probe-train size is **held fixed across the ladder** is preserved exactly; what is re-pinned is its study-wide value, and it is pinned before any targeted-factor look. The interpolation-split readout is retained and co-reported so the change is visible rather than substitutive. Root cause is recorded for the paper: both datasets are **complete factorial grids** split by random index, so probe-test is dense lattice interpolation that an untrained CNN plus a 164k-parameter probe already solves.

**(d) H4 gains the random-projector floor its statistic assumes.** §6 H4 specifies paired `G(encoder) − G(projector)`. The implementation computes `trained_h − trained_z` with the annotation that the floor cancels. It does not cancel: no random-**projector** stack exists (the sweep's `random` array is backbone-only), so the statistic is a raw level difference between a 512-d space and a 128-d space and is confounded by dimensionality. The sweep now extracts a **matched untrained projector** on the same random-encoder seeds, and H4 is computed as specified. H4's descriptive-sign-only status under A1 is unchanged.

**(e) A6(b) capacity-axis validity — discharged affirmatively, plus one cross-check.** A6(b) promised a capacity-axis validity check against Lee & Kondor (2026). It is discharged here as an **affirmative** result rather than a caveat: the ladder is a **nested function-class hierarchy**, `linear ⊂ mlp_small ⊂ mlp_large ⊂ mlp_deep`, because `ReLU(a) − ReLU(−a) = a` lets any rung represent its predecessor exactly whenever `2 · out_dim ≤` the next hidden width, which holds at every step here (out_dim ≤ 4; widths 64, 256, 128). Recoverability is therefore monotone in capacity by construction up to optimization error, and parameter count orders a genuine inclusion chain rather than serving as a proxy for one. The residual risk is **optimization**, not expressivity, and is now measured: the study contains two implementations of the same linear-rung quantity, and on the random encoder's shape readout they disagree by **0.079** (0.9082 via the closed-form/sklearn probe, 0.8290 via the ladder's 100-epoch Adam probe). Since `Δ_G` differences the linear rung against the top rung, an optimizer-limited linear rung inflates it. The ladder's linear rung is therefore **cross-checked against a closed-form/convex solver on the identical features**, and both are reported; a disagreement above 0.02 at the linear rung is disclosed per readout.

**Consistency.** (a), (b)-gate and (c)-(2) change decision rules and are filed **before any verdict exists**, which is the only condition under which such changes are legitimate; (b)-control, (c)-(1), (c)-(3), (d) and (e) repair implementations to match what §3/§4/§6 already specify, or add co-reported diagnostics. Nothing here narrows a family, relaxes a threshold, or removes a cell: (a) makes ε_G **larger** and therefore invariance verdicts **easier** to obtain, and (b) makes the §5 gate **harder** to fail. Both move against the direction that would flatter the study's hypotheses.

## 0. Locked design parameters

- **Datasets:** **Shapes3D primary**, **dSprites secondary**. (MPI3D optional later confirmation only.)
- **Factors + types (Shapes3D):** floor hue, wall hue, object hue, scale, orientation = **continuous** (R²); shape = **categorical** (normalized accuracy). dSprites supplies **x/y position** (continuous) for the position arm.
- **Headline contrast:** object hue (color-jitter, expected-genuine) vs. x/y position (random-crop, expected-artifact).
- **Augmentation conditions × strengths:** Color (hue/color jitter), Position (crop/translation), Orientation (rotation), Scale (random-resized-crop), + Control-aug (minimal); each at **≥2 strengths** (weak, strong).
- **Probe-capacity ladder:** (1) linear · (2) MLP 1-hidden small · (3) MLP 1-hidden large · (4) MLP 2-hidden. **Monotone capacity; capacity reported as an explicit measure (param count / effective DOF) per rung.** Exact widths fixed at probe-build (not a frozen quantity).
- **Seeds:** **≥10 per cell**, seed controls **both** encoder and probe init; bootstrap over seeds. Cell = `(factor, condition, strength, probe_capacity, seed)`.
- **Probe discipline:** probe-train / val / probe-test split; **probe-train size held FIXED across the ladder**; regularization tuned on val, **never** on test; report probe-test only.

## 1. Generative model / dataset
Factor-controlled synthetic datasets where each image's generative factors are known **by construction**. Shapes3D (6 factors above) primary; dSprites (shape, scale, orientation, x, y) secondary.

## 2. "True factor value"
The ground-truth generative factor value for each image, **defined by the dataset's construction, not estimated**. Each factor is the probe's prediction target.

## 3. Recoverability, encoder gain, probe selectivity (the metric layer)

- **Recoverability `R(F,c)`** — probe-**test** performance predicting factor F from the **frozen encoder** at capacity c, on a **normalized [0,1]** scale: categorical → `(acc−chance)/(1−chance)`; continuous → `R²`. **R²<0 retained, not clipped** (clipping biases G upward). ⚠️ orientation is cyclic → circular target / sin–cos at probe-build.
- **Probe selectivity `S(F,c) = R(real labels) − R(random labels)`** — Hewitt–Liang **random-LABEL** control task. Bounds the *probe* (S≈0 ⇒ probe is memorizing).
- **Encoder gain `G(F,c) = R(trained encoder) − R(random ENCODER)`** — matched-architecture untrained baseline. Bounds *representation-free* structure. **G is the headline metric.**
- **Commensurability (FIX 1):** normalized-accuracy and R² are different units. **G is interpreted per-factor** against that factor's own ε_G; cross-factor comparisons restricted to **within-type** factor pairs — R² and normalized-accuracy are never pooled or rank-compared.
- **Capacity gap `Δ_G(F) = G(F, top rung) − G(F, linear)`.**

## 4. "Invariance" (ε-invariance) — the dual-gate

- **Invariance verdict boolean (also the flip-count boolean):** trained encoder is **ε-invariant to F at capacity c iff `G(F,c) ≤ ε_G`**.
- **Genuine recovery requires BOTH gates:** `G(F,c) > ε_G` **AND** `S(F,c) > 0`. Three exhaustive cases (FIX 3):
  - `G ≤ ε_G` → **invariant**; the sub-case `G < 0` ("below-random / actively suppressed") is reported **distinctly**, not merged with "indistinguishable from random."
  - `G > ε_G, S > 0` → **genuine recovery** (a *linear-invariance artifact* when it appears only above the linear rung).
  - `G > ε_G, S ≤ 0` → **inconclusive / probe-driven** (dead zone): not genuine recovery; flagged separately. The invariance boolean keys on G alone, so the flip-count is well-defined.
- **ε_G primary = data-derived per-factor:** upper bound of the bootstrap 95% CI of G under the **random-vs-random-encoder null** (captures **encoder-init noise only**). Capacity-dependent probe over-fitting is controlled by **S**; **both gates use the same paired seed bootstrap**; the AND-gate is a conjunction of two one-sided tests, each component reported. **Fixed ε_G = 0.05 normalized = sensitivity analysis only** (FIX 2, 5).

## 5. Controls
- **Random-LABEL control task** → defines S (bounds probe).
- **Random-ENCODER control** (matched arch, untrained) → defines G (bounds representation-free structure).
- **Supervised-reference encoder** → recoverability ceiling; **diagnostic-only** (not in any decision rule).
- **Encoder-quality gate** → downstream linear-probe accuracy proving the encoder is well-trained **before** any invariance claim.

## 6. Hypotheses (LOCKED) + confirm/refute rules

All tests on **encoder gain G** with **probe selectivity S as the recovery gate**, **≥10 seeds**, bootstrap CIs, **Wilcoxon signed-rank** for paired capacity/factor comparisons, effect sizes with CIs, **Holm** correction across factor×condition cells. Probe-test only.

- **H1 (capacity dependence).** G(F,·) is non-decreasing in capacity and rises materially above linear for ≥1 factor.
  - **Confirm:** capacity gap `Δ_G(F)` bootstrap CI **> 0 and > ε_G** for ≥1 factor (with S>0 at the top rung). **Refute:** all `Δ_G` CIs include 0 or stay ≤ ε_G.
- **H2 (heterogeneity).** The capacity effect differs across factors.
  - **Confirm:** **paired per-seed difference** of `Δ_G` for ≥1 **within-type** factor pair has a Wilcoxon-significant / bootstrap-CI-excludes-0 difference (Holm across pairs). **Refute:** all within-type difference CIs include 0. *(FIX 4 — replaces the earlier non-overlapping-CI test.)*
- **H3 (genuine invariance via control).** Some factors stay invariant at every capacity.
  - **Confirm (existence):** ≥1 factor has `G(F,c) ≤ ε_G` at **every** rung (selectivity CI within ±ε_G across the ladder). **Refute:** no factor stays ε_G-invariant across all capacities.
- **H4 (encoder-vs-projector localization — a TEST of Cosentino et al. 2022, not a discovery).** Invariance concentrates in the projector.
  - **Confirm:** paired **`G(encoder) − G(projector) > 0`** for targeted factors (Wilcoxon, Holm), **widening with augmentation strength**. **Refute:** difference CI includes 0 / negative.

**Headline number — verdict-stability flip count:** # of (factor, condition) cells where the boolean `G ≤ ε_G` changes between the linear probe and the top rung; reported for **both** primary per-factor ε_G and the 0.05 sensitivity ε_G.

## 7. Analysis plan (LOCKED)
- Bootstrap CIs on G, S, and Δ_G across seeds; **paired seed resampling** shared by the G and S gates.
- Effect sizes with CIs for the capacity gap (not just p-values).
- Wilcoxon signed-rank across seeds for all paired comparisons (H1 capacity, H2 within-type differences, H4 encoder−projector).
- **Holm** correction across the factor×condition cell family; state the family explicitly.
- Report **per-cell**; never collapse to a single average that hides heterogeneity (the heterogeneity is the finding).
- **Power caveat (pre-registered):** Wilcoxon at n=10 has limited power for small effects after Holm; a pilot power check may raise seeds before the full sweep.
- **AI-assistance disclosure:** literature search and drafting used AI tools; recorded for transparency.

## 8. Deferred to later phases (NOT frozen quantities)
Exact probe widths (probe-build); the capacity measure plotted against G (param count vs. effective DOF — pre-register at probe-build, engaging Lee & Kondor 2026); orientation circular-regression handling; real-data sanity-check dataset.

### A5 — 2026-07-28 — Shapes3D Orientation arm excluded from the confirmatory grid (augmentation–factor construct mismatch)

**Unblinding status at the time of this amendment:** unchanged from A2–A4 — the H1–H4 statistics layer, flip counts, ε_G, and figures have **not** been run on any real cell, and no targeted-factor verdict has been inspected. This amendment rests **solely on the §5 encoder-quality gate**, computed from the augmentation-**preserved** factor (object **shape**) and by construction orthogonal to every targeted-factor hypothesis (H1–H4 concern the *targeted* factors, never shape). No orientation-recoverability result was examined in drafting it.

**Issue — the Orientation augmentation does not manipulate the Orientation factor.** The Shapes3D orientation factor is generated by **camera azimuth**: a viewpoint change (on SO(3)) that reveals *different faces* of the object. The Orientation-arm augmentation is **2D in-plane image rotation** (SO(2) acting on the projected image and its floor/wall backdrop, object face unchanged). These are distinct group actions, and invariance to one does not entail invariance to the other, so the arm trains invariance to a transform that is **not** the generative factor it is subsequently probed on; a low orientation-recoverability reading from this arm would be uninterpretable as azimuth invariance.

**Corroborating evidence (gate only).** Image-space rotation wide enough to span the factor's ±30° support drives the preserved shape anchor **below the untrained random-encoder floor** (≈0.83), i.e. the encoder fails the §5 trustworthiness criterion: linear-rung shape recoverability = **0.65–0.77 at ±180°** (full 12-seed sweep, 0/12 gate pass; `results/probes/orientation_strong/meta.json`) and **0.80–0.85 at ±30°** (bounded to the exact factor range; subsampled linear-rung check, 3 seeds, with training diagnostics homogeneous across all 12 seeds). No image-rotation setting resolves this: any arc wide enough to cover the ±30° factor destroys shape, while a narrower arc under-manipulates the factor. The failure is **structural (a construct mismatch), not a tunable hyperparameter**.

**Change.** The **Shapes3D Orientation arm (`orientation_strong`) is removed from the realized confirmatory grid** — excluded from the H1, H2, and H3 families and from the headline verdict-stability flip count — and reported as a **labeled methodological limitation** (the gate failure and its construct-validity cause), not as a recoverability row (no gate-passing encoder exists to source one). This narrows the realized grid (A1) from five strong cells to four — **Color, Position, Scale, and Control-aug**.

**Scope — what is and isn't touched.** (1) The exclusion is **arm-level, not readout-level**: it removes the `orientation_strong` *cell*. This is distinct from A3, which removed the `(dsprites, orientation)` *readout column* everywhere — here the orientation **factor remains a valid confirmatory readout in the other Shapes3D cells** (Color, Scale, Control), where the encoder was not trained with rotation augmentation. (2) **No decision rule changes** — every surviving G/ε_G verdict, selectivity, flip count, and test is computed identically; only the realized-grid membership narrows (stated explicitly per §6). (3) Removing the arm drops its targeted comparisons from the H2 within-type and flip families; this **reduces** the multiple-comparison burden on the surviving factors and is disclosed as a direct consequence of removing an untrustworthy arm, **not** a post-hoc power adjustment. (4) H4 is affected only in that orientation is no longer an H4-targeted factor (its `TARGETED_FACTORS` entry is inert with the cell absent); H4's "partial/descriptive" status (A1) is otherwise unchanged. **Implementation:** `orientation_strong` is removed from `hypotheses.EXPECTED_CELLS`; the §5 gate already excludes the arm's 0/12 encoders downstream, so this makes the exclusion principled and disclosed rather than incidental.

**Consistency.** This applies the logic of A1 (principled, pre-data scope curtailment of the realized grid) and A3 (exclusion of a construct-problematic orientation readout) to the Shapes3D case. Orientation has now proven un-manipulable-without-confound on **both** datasets — itself a reported finding on the correspondence between image-space augmentations and 3D generative factors.

### A8 — 2026-08-07 — Metric-layer repairs (ε_G, the random-label control, null-headroom, H4's floor) and the A6(b) capacity-axis discharge

**Unblinding status at the time of this amendment:** unchanged from A2–A7 — the H1–H4 statistics layer, ε_G, flip counts, verdicts, and figures have **not** been run on any real cell, and **no targeted-factor recoverability value has been inspected**. Every change below rests on (i) analysis of the estimator code itself, (ii) simulation on synthetic draws, (iii) the random-**encoder** floor levels disclosed in A4 (D015/D019), and (iv) the shape anchor. This amendment is filed **because** the confirmatory layer has never run: these are defects that can only be corrected honestly before a verdict exists, and this is the last moment at which that is true.

**(a) ε_G is redefined as the null *quantile*, not a confidence interval of the null *mean*. (Decision-rule change.)** Frozen §4 specifies ε_G as the "upper bound of the bootstrap 95% CI of G under the random-vs-random-encoder null," and `instrument.epsilon_g` implements exactly that: it pools all ordered seed pairs `R_i − R_j` **and** `R_j − R_i`, then bootstraps the **mean** of that pool. The pool is antisymmetric, so its mean is **identically zero for any data whatsoever**, and the bootstrap therefore returns the standard error of an estimator of zero rather than the spread of the null gain. Quantified on synthetic draws at per-seed σ = 0.05 with S = 12: ε_G = **0.0126**, where the true 97.5th percentile of the null gain distribution is **0.1329** (a 10.5× understatement) and the sampling SE of the paired G being thresholded is **0.0204** — the threshold is *smaller than the noise in the statistic it gates*. It is also **a function of the control sample size**, shrinking as 1/√(S(S−1)): 0.0126 at S = 12, 0.0055 at S = 24, 0.0030 at S = 48. Adding random-encoder seeds would therefore make readouts *more* likely to be called "recovered", which inverts the intended direction and makes the headline unreplicable at a different seed budget. The defect is in the **frozen definition**, not in its implementation, so it is corrected here rather than silently. **New primary:** `ε_G(F,c) = the (1 − α/2) empirical quantile of the random-vs-random null gain distribution` (α = 0.05), which is what §4's own gloss — "how large a gain init noise alone can produce" — describes. **Demoted to co-reported sensitivity, not removed:** the frozen CI-of-the-mean reading, and the fixed ε_G = 0.05 already specified in §4. All three are reported side by side for every readout, and the primary is named in every table.

**(b) The random-label control task (S) is repaired, and the §5 gate becomes three-valued.** *Control task.* `instrument.permute_labels` shuffles each split's label rows **independently**, so the control label is not a function of the image and the control task is unlearnable at probe-test **by construction**; consequently `R(random labels) ≈ chance`, `S ≈ R(real)` always, `S > 0` is satisfied whenever a factor is recoverable at all, and §4's third case (`G > ε_G, S ≤ 0`, the probe-driven dead zone) is unreachable. That is not the Hewitt–Liang control task §3/§9 cite, whose type→label map is held **fixed across splits** precisely so a high-capacity probe *can* fit it. **New definition:** the control task is a random permutation of each factor's **value→target map**, drawn once per (factor, seed) and applied **identically to probe-train, probe-val, and probe-test**. Every factor in both datasets is discrete-valued, so this is well-defined. Disclosed consequence, stated rather than hidden: for a **categorical** factor a value permutation is a pure relabeling, so `S ≡ 0` identically and the dual gate degenerates to a single gate on categorical readouts. This is harmless for the study as realized, because **every targeted factor is continuous** (hue, position, scale, orientation) and shape — the only categorical factor — is the gate anchor, never a targeted readout. For continuous factors the permutation destroys the target's ordinal/metric structure, so S measures exactly what §3 intends. *Gate.* The §5 gate's **PASS** direction is unchanged and remains sound: linear decodability of shape at ≥ 0.90 is sufficient evidence of a non-collapsed encoder, and presence needs no capacity argument. Its **FAIL** direction, however, commits the inference this study exists to indict — non-decodability at the linear rung is precisely the ambiguous evidence the capacity ladder is built to disambiguate. The gate is therefore **three-valued** from here: `PASS` unchanged; `FAIL-DESTROYED` when the trained-minus-floor anchor deficit is negative at **every** rung and does not close with capacity (this licenses exclusion); `FAIL-INDETERMINATE` otherwise (a linear-readout collapse, which does **not** license exclusion on gate grounds alone). Applied retrospectively: Scale is `FAIL-DESTROYED`, so A7's exclusion is gate-licensed. Orientation is `FAIL-INDETERMINATE`, so **A5's "Corroborating evidence (gate only)" paragraph is retro-labeled non-load-bearing**; A5's exclusion stands on its analytic SO(2)-versus-SO(3) construct argument, which requires no measurement, and the reader is told so explicitly.

**(c) Null-headroom: a co-primary headroom-normalized gain, and a calibrated instrument.** A4 §a defined null-saturation and A6 §a promoted it to a gate on H3 and the flip count. The disclosed A4 random-**encoder** floors make the consequence concrete: at the **linear** rung on Shapes3D the floors are floor_hue 0.9890, wall_hue 0.9892, object_hue 0.9275, scale 0.9122, shape 0.9082, orientation 0.9804, so the maximum attainable G is between **+0.011 and +0.092** and shrinks further at higher rungs. Under A6 §a's rule (floor ≥ 0.90 at the linear **or** top rung) **all six Shapes3D readouts are saturated at the linear rung**, including `object_hue`, the left-hand side of the §0 headline contrast, so the saturation-gated headline family is empty on Shapes3D and the flip count would report 0 over **zero eligible readouts**. A null produced by the instrument is not a finding. Three additive changes, no relaxation of A6 §a: (1) **co-primary headroom-normalized gain** `G̃(F,c) = G(F,c) / (1 − R̄_random(F,c))`, a per-readout monotone rescaling of G that expresses gain as a fraction of the headroom actually available, reported alongside raw G everywhere and with the same paired-seed bootstrap; (2) **an empty saturation-gated family raises rather than silently reporting zero** — the count is emitted as `undefined` with the eligible-readout set printed; (3) **instrument headroom calibration**, run **blind** on the shape anchor and the A4 §b raw-pixel reference ladder only (never on a targeted factor): the probe-train size and a **held-out-factor-value (extrapolation) probe split** are swept, and the configuration is pinned to the one where the random-encoder floor stays below the A4 saturation level at the top rung on both datasets. The §0 discipline that probe-train size is **held fixed across the ladder** is preserved exactly; what is re-pinned is its study-wide value, and it is pinned before any targeted-factor look. The interpolation-split readout is retained and co-reported so the change is visible rather than substitutive. Root cause is recorded for the paper: both datasets are **complete factorial grids** split by random index, so probe-test is dense lattice interpolation that an untrained CNN plus a 164k-parameter probe already solves.

**(d) H4 gains the random-projector floor its statistic assumes.** §6 H4 specifies paired `G(encoder) − G(projector)`. The implementation computes `trained_h − trained_z` with the annotation that the floor cancels. It does not cancel: no random-**projector** stack exists (the sweep's `random` array is backbone-only), so the statistic is a raw level difference between a 512-d space and a 128-d space and is confounded by dimensionality. The sweep now extracts a **matched untrained projector** on the same random-encoder seeds, and H4 is computed as specified. H4's descriptive-sign-only status under A1 is unchanged.

**(e) A6(b) capacity-axis validity — discharged affirmatively, plus one cross-check.** A6(b) promised a capacity-axis validity check against Lee & Kondor (2026). It is discharged here as an **affirmative** result rather than a caveat: the ladder is a **nested function-class hierarchy**, `linear ⊂ mlp_small ⊂ mlp_large ⊂ mlp_deep`, because `ReLU(a) − ReLU(−a) = a` lets any rung represent its predecessor exactly whenever `2 · out_dim ≤` the next hidden width, which holds at every step here (out_dim ≤ 4; widths 64, 256, 128). Recoverability is therefore monotone in capacity by construction up to optimization error, and parameter count orders a genuine inclusion chain rather than serving as a proxy for one. The residual risk is **optimization**, not expressivity, and is now measured: the study contains two implementations of the same linear-rung quantity, and on the random encoder's shape readout they disagree by **0.079** (0.9082 via the closed-form/sklearn probe, 0.8290 via the ladder's 100-epoch Adam probe). Since `Δ_G` differences the linear rung against the top rung, an optimizer-limited linear rung inflates it. The ladder's linear rung is therefore **cross-checked against a closed-form/convex solver on the identical features**, and both are reported; a disagreement above 0.02 at the linear rung is disclosed per readout.

**Consistency.** (a), (b)-gate and (c)-(2) change decision rules and are filed **before any verdict exists**, which is the only condition under which such changes are legitimate; (b)-control, (c)-(1), (c)-(3), (d) and (e) repair implementations to match what §3/§4/§6 already specify, or add co-reported diagnostics. Nothing here narrows a family, relaxes a threshold, or removes a cell: (a) makes ε_G **larger** and therefore invariance verdicts **easier** to obtain, and (b) makes the §5 gate **harder** to fail. Both move against the direction that would flatter the study's hypotheses.

### A9 — 2026-08-11 — Probe optimization budget pinned in gradient STEPS, not epochs

**Unblinding status:** unchanged from A2–A8 — the H1–H4 layer has **not** been run on any real cell and **no targeted-factor recoverability value has been inspected**. This amendment rests entirely on the A8 §c calibration run, which by construction scores only the **shape** anchor and the raw-pixel reference.

**Issue, found by the A8 §e cross-check on its first execution.** `ladder._train_one` ran a fixed **epoch** count at a fixed batch size, so the gradient-step budget scaled with probe-train size: 100 epochs × ⌈n/4096⌉ gives **100 steps at n = 2000 but 1000 at n = 40000**. Probe-train size and optimizer budget were therefore perfectly correlated, and the A8 §c headroom sweep could not distinguish "a smaller probe-train set makes the task genuinely harder, lowering the random-encoder floor" from "the probe was given ten times fewer steps and failed to converge." Observed on the first calibration run (Shapes3D, shape anchor, random encoder): top-rung floor 0.7872 at n = 2000 and 0.8762 at n = 5000, i.e. apparently ample headroom — while the A8 §e closed-form cross-check simultaneously reported a linear-rung disagreement of **+0.1608** and **+0.1451** against the ladder's Adam probe, eight times the 0.02 disclosure tolerance. The sweep's own validity check caught its own confound. Left uncorrected, the study would have pinned a probe configuration whose measured headroom was an optimization artifact — reintroducing, inside the instrument, precisely the absent-versus-inaccessible conflation the study exists to expose.

**Change (instrument fidelity, not a decision rule).** The probe optimization budget is pinned as a **gradient-step target** (`ladder.DEFAULT_STEPS = 1000`), with the batch capped at the probe-train size and the epoch count derived so every probe-train size receives the same ~1000 steps. §0's discipline that probe-train size is **held fixed across the ladder**, and that regularization is the only per-rung knob, is preserved exactly; what is now also held fixed is the optimizer budget **across probe-train sizes**, which the epoch-based formulation silently varied. The value 1000 is chosen to reproduce the pre-existing n = 40000 behaviour, so every sweep already run at that size is unchanged and remains comparable. Separately, the closed-form reference solver's iteration cap is raised (1000 → 5000) because it reported non-convergence on the 12288-d pixel features, which made the reported +0.16 gap a **lower** bound.

**Consequence and reporting.** (1) The first A8 §c calibration run is **void for cross-`n` comparison** and is retained only for its interpolation-versus-extrapolation contrast **at fixed n**, where the step budget is shared and the comparison is clean. The sweep is re-run under the pinned budget before any probe-train size is pinned. (2) The A8 §e linear-rung cross-check is re-evaluated after the fix; if the gap does not fall below 0.02, the ladder's linear rung is reported as optimizer-limited and every `Δ_G` is co-reported against the closed-form linear rung. (3) A uniform-protocol note for the paper: all four rungs keep the same optimizer, schedule and step budget, so the capacity axis remains a clean function-class ordering (A8 §e) rather than a confound between expressivity and optimization.

### A10 — 2026-08-12 — Probe target normalization (all continuous readouts void); all-factor headroom in the calibration; two-sided invariance boolean

**Unblinding status at the time of this amendment:** unchanged from A2–A9. The two code repairs in (a) and (b) were made before this amendment was filed and before any confirmatory run.

**(a) The continuous-target readout was optimizer-broken, and every continuous recoverability value computed to date is void. (Instrument fidelity, not a decision rule.)** `ladder._standardize` standardized the **features**; the **target** was passed to `MSELoss` raw, the probe's output bias was initialized near zero, and `Adam(probe.parameters(), weight_decay=wd)` applied weight decay to that bias. A probe therefore had to reach the target mean through a decayed bias inside the A9 step budget, which is not possible when the target's mean is large relative to its spread. Measured on the **random-encoder** array at the linear rung (n = 40000, 12 seeds): dSprites `pos_x` **R² = −1.51**, `pos_y` **−1.55**, dSprites `orientation` **−2.49**, Shapes3D `scale` **−1.97**, while the Shapes3D hues (mean 0.45 on a [0, 0.9] support) sat at 0.97. A random CNN certainly encodes sprite position linearly, so these are not representation properties. Reproduced under controlled conditions on a **perfectly linear** synthetic task whose true capacity gap is exactly zero: the ladder returned an apparent linear-to-top gap of **+0.143** at target scale 17 and **+1.498** at target mean 3.14. After the repair the same task returns **−0.0005** at every target mean and scale, with the linear rung at R² = 1.0000.

- **Repair.** Continuous targets are centered and scaled on **probe-train** statistics (`ladder._standardize_targets`); R² is invariant under an affine map applied to both target and prediction, so the reported metric is unchanged in definition. Biases are placed in a `weight_decay = 0` parameter group. Categorical readouts are untouched.
- **Direction of the error.** The artifact fell hardest on the **linear rung**, because the output bias is the only route to the target mean there, whereas a hidden ReLU layer offers many cheaper ones. It therefore **inflated `Δ_G`, the H1 statistic, in the direction that confirms H1**, and it did not cancel in `G`: the artifact's size depends on the weight norm the probe needs, which is exactly what differs between the trained and random encoders.
- **Consequence, stated without hedging.** All continuous-factor entries of `results/probes/{color,control,position}_strong/stacks.npz` are **void** and are recomputed before any confirmatory run. Categorical (`shape`) entries are unaffected, so the §5 gate outcomes, and with them A5's and A7's exclusions, stand.
- **Second consequence, disclosed because it moves against the study.** The broken readout **depressed the random-encoder floor** on precisely the factors it hit, in places below zero, so it *overstated* available headroom. Repairing it will **raise** those floors and make the A6(a) null-saturation problem more general, not less. This is disclosed in advance of the recalibration rather than reported as a surprise afterwards.

**(b) The calibration's blind guard is corrected, and a pin now requires headroom on every factor.** A8 §c's headroom calibration scored the categorical **shape** anchor alone (`instrument_calibration._anchor_only`), and A9's pin (`probe_train = 2000`, `steps = 1000`, interpolation) rests on that anchor. The guard was stricter than the blind requires: the module builds only **random encoders** and the identity pixel reference and never loads a trained checkpoint, so no quantity it computes can carry a trained-encoder value, and A4 already discloses random-encoder floors. Meanwhile the anchor is not representative: at n = 40000 the anchor's top-rung floor is 0.9858 while `floor_hue` and `wall_hue` reach 0.9982 and 0.9986. **A pin validated on shape can leave every targeted readout saturated.** The runner now scores the floor on **every** factor for the random and pixel roles (`--all-factor-floors`, default on), reports `factor_floor_mean`, `worst_top_rung_floor`, `saturated_factors_at_top` and `all_factor_headroom_ok` per row, and `recommended_config` requires **no factor saturated at the top rung** in addition to the existing A8 §e linear-rung agreement. **A9's pin is therefore void as a study-wide pin** and is re-derived from the recalibration under the repaired ladder. If no interpolation configuration clears the all-factor gate, the **composition** regime is the declared fallback; adopting it changes the estimand from lattice interpolation to compositional generalization and requires its own dated amendment, which is not filed here.

**(c) The invariance boolean and the flip statistic become two-sided. (Decision-rule change.)** Frozen §4 defines ε-invariance as `G(F,c) ≤ ε_G`, one-sided, and `hypotheses._INVARIANT_CASES` consequently pools the `G ≈ 0` case ("indistinguishable from the untrained floor") with the `G < 0` case ("suppressed below it"). The headline verdict-stability flip then requires `case_top == "genuine"`, i.e. `G(top) > ε_G`. Computed from the **random-encoder** array of the existing `color_strong` stack, at the top rung: `object_hue` floor 0.9929, so the **maximum attainable `G` is 0.0071**, against ε_G = 0.0388 under A8(a)'s null quantile. For `floor_hue` and `wall_hue` the ceiling on `G` is 0.0018 and 0.0014. **The `genuine` case is unreachable, so the headline flip count is pinned at zero for the §0 headline factor independently of anything the encoders learned.** A statistic that cannot fire is not a conservative test; it is a construct failure. The direction of the frozen test is also wrong for the question: `G > ε_G` asks whether training **added** linearly accessible information, whereas an invariance study asks whether training **removed** it.

- **New primary definitions.** With the training-induced deficit `D(F,c) = R(random encoder) − R(trained encoder)` and `ε_D(F,c)` the (1 − α/2) empirical quantile of the random-vs-random null **deficit** distribution (α = 0.05, the same estimator and the same paired seed bootstrap A8(a) fixed for ε_G):
  - **suppressed at capacity c** iff `D(F,c) > ε_D`;
  - **recovered at capacity c** iff `D(F,c) ≤ ε_D`;
  - **ε-invariant** iff suppressed at **every** rung;
  - **verdict-stability flip** iff suppressed at the linear rung **and** recovered at the top rung.

  Note `Δ_G = D(F, linear) − D(F, top)` identically, so H1's statistic is unchanged; only the boolean built on top of it changes. The A8(c) co-primary headroom-normalized `G̃` and the A1 §c absolute-level co-reporting persist unchanged.
- **Direction check, and why this is legitimate anyway.** Unlike A8(a) and A8(b), this change moves **toward** the study's hypotheses: it makes flips possible where they were impossible. It is filed regardless because (i) it is a construct repair, not a threshold relaxation, and it is filed **before any verdict exists**, which is the only condition under which such a change is legitimate; (ii) the new gate is not weaker but differently directed, since `recovered` now requires a real deficit to have closed, whereas the frozen `invariant` was satisfiable by a readout sitting at a near-ceiling floor having done nothing; (iii) both the suppression and the closure conditions carry their own data-derived null thresholds; and (iv) the frozen one-sided boolean and both frozen flip variants remain **co-reported as sensitivities** in every table, so a reader can see exactly what the frozen rule would have returned.
- **Scope.** H1 (keys on `Δ_G`), H2 (within-type `Δ_G` differences), H4, S, ε_G, `G̃`, the A6(a) null-saturation gate on H3, the A3/Q16 exclusion, and the A6(c) maximal-Holm-family disclosure are all **unchanged**. H3's existence claim and the headline flip count are restated on the new boolean, with the frozen versions alongside. The A7(d) ratchet stop is untouched: no cell is removed here.

**Consistency.** (a) and (b) repair the instrument to measure what §0/§3 already specify, in the same spirit as A8(b)-control, A8(d), A8(e) and A9, and both make the study's numbers less favourable. (c) is a decision-rule change of the same kind as A8(a): a frozen definition that provably cannot do the job §4 assigns it, corrected before any verdict exists and co-reported against the frozen reading. No family is narrowed, no threshold is relaxed, no cell is removed, and the confirmatory layer has still never run.

### A11 — 2026-08-13 — Counterfactual displacement spectrum preregistered; the (1 − ρ_F) → Δ_G link and its own Holm family

**Unblinding status at the time of this amendment:** unchanged from A2–A10 — the H1–H4 statistics layer, ε_G, ε_D, flip counts, verdicts and figures have **not** been run on any real cell, and **no targeted-factor recoverability value has been inspected**. No displacement statistic has been computed on any encoder, trained or random. This amendment is filed **before the instrument is run for the first time**, which is the only condition under which preregistering a link test is worth anything. `src/probes/displacement.py` refuses to load a trained checkpoint unless this amendment is named on the command line.

**Motivation.** A10(c) repaired the invariance boolean but not its dependence on a probe. Every quantity in §3–§4 is defined through a fitted probe, so "the information is absent" and "the probe could not find it" are separated only by the capacity ladder plus the random-encoder control — the very inference the study exists to interrogate. Both datasets are **complete factorial grids**, so for any factor F, values v, v′, and any assignment z of the remaining factors, `g(z, F=v)` and `g(z, F=v′)` both exist. Exact counterfactual pairs with context held fixed are therefore available **by construction**, and the encoder's response to changing F alone can be measured with **no probe of any kind**.

**(a) New preregistered quantities (probe-free; additive — no existing decision rule changes).** With `h` the frozen encoder, `W = Σ^{-1/2}` the whitening map fit on that encoder's **probe-train** embeddings (identical procedure for every role, so the statistics are affine- rather than merely orthogonally-invariant), and `Δ_F(z; v→v′) = W·(h(g(z, F=v′)) − h(g(z, F=v)))` collected over a **fixed** context sample into `M_F`:

- **displacement magnitude** `m_F = mean_z ‖Δ_F(z)‖`;
- **single-direction fraction** `ρ_F = σ₁² / Σᵢ σᵢ²`;
- **displacement effective rank** `r_F = (Σσᵢ²)² / Σσᵢ⁴`.

Estimation discipline, fixed here rather than at analysis time: (1) `σᵢ²` is biased upward at small context counts, so the singular basis is **fit on one context sample and every reported statistic is evaluated on a disjoint held-out sample**, with a percentile bootstrap resampling **contexts** (not individual displacements, which are dependent within a context); the in-sample `ρ_F` is co-reported so the size of the bias is visible. (2) Contexts are drawn from a **fixed seed** and depend only on (factor, seed, n_contexts), so the identical context set is reused across factors, encoders and roles and every comparison is paired. (3) `n_contexts = 512`, split 256 fit / 256 evaluate; the sweep runs over **all** values of F per context and displacements are consecutive-value differences.

**(b) The destruction certificate and its null.** `ε_m(F)` is the (1 − α/2) empirical quantile of the random-vs-random-encoder null on the `m_F` deficit (α = 0.05) — **the same estimator A8(a) fixed for ε_G and A10(c) for ε_D**. A factor is **DESTROYED** at an encoder iff `m_F(random) − m_F(trained) > ε_m`: the encoder maps the counterfactual pair to the same point, so **no probe of any capacity can recover F**. This is a capacity-free, probe-free certificate of genuine invariance, and it is the one claim in this study that does not inherit the absent-versus-inaccessible ambiguity. A certificate without a null is not a certificate, which is why `ε_m` is defined here and not left to analysis.

**(c) `ρ_F` bounds linear decodability — as a bound, and only as a bound.** A linear probe reads a factor off one fixed direction, so a small `ρ_F` implies a linear probe cannot do well. The converse does **not** hold: a displacement field concentrated on a single direction whose projection is **non-monotone** in v (rising then falling) is single-direction and still linearly undecodable. A **monotonicity check** is therefore preregistered alongside `ρ_F` — the fraction of contexts whose projection onto `σ₁` is monotone in the factor's ordered values, plus the mean |Spearman| over contexts — and every claim keyed on `ρ_F` is stated as a bound, never as a prediction of achievable linear performance.

**(d) The link test (the falsifiable prediction), with its OWN family.** **Prediction:** `(1 − ρ_F)` predicts `Δ_G(F)` across readouts. If it holds, the capacity gap is *explained* rather than merely measured; if it fails, that is reported as a failure and the (b) certificate stands alone.

- **Statistic:** Spearman rank correlation `ρ_s` between `(1 − ρ_F)` and `Δ_G(F)` over readouts, with a percentile bootstrap **over encoder seeds** (the paired seed axis §7 already fixes), plus the per-seed paired variant.
- **Family:** a **separate Holm family** comprising exactly the link tests — one per (dataset, encoder role) — declared here and **never pooled with the H1–H4 families**, which are unchanged. Adding this test therefore does not alter the multiple-comparison burden on any preregistered hypothesis.
- **Effect-size target, fixed in advance:** **confirm iff `ρ_s ≥ 0.5` and the bootstrap 95% CI excludes 0**, Holm-corrected within the link family. `ρ_s < 0.5`, or a CI containing 0, is a **refutation** and is reported as one. A CI excluding 0 at `ρ_s < 0.5` is reported as a **detected but immaterial** association, not a confirmation — the point of naming a magnitude in advance.
- **Readout eligibility:** the A3 non-identifiability exclusion and the A6(a) null-saturation gate apply unchanged, because `Δ_G` is the dependent variable and both gates bear on it. `ρ_F` itself is immune to null saturation (it computes no accuracy and has no ceiling), and that asymmetry is disclosed rather than exploited: eligibility is decided by the `Δ_G` side.

**(e) Status and scope.** The (b) certificate and the (a) descriptive statistics are **confirmatory** under this amendment. The (d) link test is **confirmatory but singly-powered**: it is one test on the realized three-cell grid, so a confirmation is reported as consistent-with, never as established, and replication on a second dataset family is named as required future work rather than claimed. Prereg §0's realized-grid membership, the A7(d) ratchet stop, and every H1–H4 rule are untouched.

**Consistency.** This is additive: it introduces new quantities and one new test with its own family, changes no existing decision rule, narrows no family, removes no cell, and is filed before a single displacement number exists. Its direction with respect to the study's hypotheses is **not** predetermined — `ρ_F` near 1 on a targeted factor would undercut the capacity story as readily as support it — which is what makes preregistering it meaningful rather than ceremonial.

### A12 — 2026-08-13 — A11's destruction certificate and its linear-readability bound are corrected (both wrong as filed)

**Unblinding status:** unchanged from A2–A11. The H1–H4 layer has **not** run on any real cell, **no targeted-factor recoverability value has been inspected**, and **no displacement statistic has been computed on any encoder, trained or random**. A11 was filed on 2026-08-13 and is corrected here the same day, before its instrument was ever run. Everything below rests on analysis of the estimator plus synthetic draws with known ground truth.

**(a) A11(b)'s certificate tested reduction, not destruction. (Decision-rule change.)** A11(b) defined a factor as DESTROYED iff `m_F(random) − m_F(trained) > ε_m`, with `ε_m` the random-vs-random null quantile. That is a **relative** statistic, and it fires on encoders that have destroyed nothing. Demonstrated on a synthetic encoder whose embedding is `h = z + v·u` — the factor is a **pure linear ramp along one axis, recoverable exactly by a linear probe** — against an untrained encoder with twice the displacement: the deficit is 0.056 against `ε_m ≈ 0.000`, so the rule returns **DESTROYED** for a perfectly decodable factor. A certificate that fires on a linearly-decodable factor is not a certificate. The defect is the same *class* as A8(a) and A10(c) — a threshold that cannot do the job its own gloss describes — and is caught here for the same reason: the instrument had not yet run.

- **New primary.** Whitening already supplies the absolute scale A11(b) lacked: after it the embedding has unit variance in **every** direction, so a whitened displacement is measured in standard deviations of the representation itself. With `m_total(F) = E_z‖Δ_F(z)‖` in whitened units, a factor is **DESTROYED** iff `m_total ≪ 1`, i.e. the counterfactual pair lands inside the representation's own noise ball in every direction. No probe of any capacity can separate points it cannot distinguish from noise; and because §1's generative factors are sampled **independently**, `h` is then a function of the context alone and carries no information about F. **Factor independence is what makes the certificate airtight**, and it is precisely what a correlated-latent setting cannot supply.
- **`ε_m` is retained but demoted.** It is reported as a **reduction** test — "does this encoder move less than an untrained one" — never as a destruction verdict. Retaining it costs nothing and shows the reader exactly what the superseded rule would have returned.
- **Why a variance share was rejected.** The natural-looking alternative, `Var_between_F / Var_total`, divides by the context variance summed over **all** dimensions, and a perfectly decodable factor in a 512-d embedding scores ≈ 0.0004 under it. A probe picks its own direction, so the noise that bounds it is the context spread **along the displacement**, which whitening already fixes at 1. The scale is therefore a whitened magnitude, not a share.

**(b) `ρ_F` does not bound linear decodability; the shared-direction component does. (Correction of a stated claim.)** A11(a)/(c) described `ρ_F = σ₁²/Σσᵢ²` as bounding linear readout quality, with a monotonicity check as the caveat. That is wrong in a way the monotonicity check does not catch: two contexts displacing by `+u` and `−u` put **all** energy on one direction (`ρ_F = 1`) while cancelling exactly, so no single fixed readout sees them, and each context's projection is perfectly monotone. `ρ_F` measures spectral **concentration**; linear readability needs direction **consistency across contexts**. **New quantity:** `m_shared(F) = ‖E_z Δ_F(z)‖` (whitened), with `ρ_shared = m_shared² / E_z‖Δ_F‖²`. A linear probe applies one fixed `w` to every context, so `m_shared` is what bounds it. `ρ_F`, `r_F` and the monotonicity check are **retained as co-reported diagnostics** of the field's geometry; they are no longer described as bounds.

**(c) The A11(d) link test is restated on the corrected quantity.** The preregistered prediction becomes **`(1 − ρ_shared)` predicts `Δ_G`**, with the identical statistic (Spearman `ρ_s` over eligible readouts), the identical **separate Holm family**, and the identical fixed effect-size target (**confirm iff `ρ_s ≥ 0.5` and the bootstrap 95% CI excludes 0**; CI-excludes-0 below 0.5 remains *detected but immaterial*). The `(1 − ρ_F)` version is co-reported as a sensitivity. This is a correction of which quantity instantiates the pre-existing hypothesis, not a change to the hypothesis, the family, or the target — all three were fixed in A11 and are untouched.

**Scope.** H1, H2, H3, H4, `S`, `ε_G`, `ε_D`, `G̃`, the A6(a) null-saturation gate, the A3 exclusion, the A10(c) two-sided boolean and the A7(d) ratchet stop are **unchanged**. No family narrows, no threshold relaxes, no cell is removed.

**Direction check.** (a) makes the destruction certificate **strictly harder** to obtain: the frozen rule fired on a halved-but-decodable factor, the new one requires the pair to be genuinely inseparable. (b) replaces a bound that was too permissive with one that is correct, and `ρ_shared ≤ ρ_F` in the cases that matter, so the link test's predictor becomes more conservative too. Both move **against** the study's hypotheses. The confirmatory layer has still never run.

### A13 — 2026-08-13 — Link test gains per-seed power; closure fraction κ; ground-truth destruction control; external-encoder check

**Unblinding status:** unchanged from A2–A12. The H1–H4 layer has **not** run on any real cell, **no targeted-factor recoverability value has been inspected**, and **no displacement statistic has been computed on any encoder**. Filed before stage 7 of the run pipeline executes.

**(a) The A11(d)/A12(c) link test is computed per SEED, not once over readouts. (Power, not direction.)** As filed, the link statistic was a single Spearman over eligible readouts. On the A7(d) three-cell realized grid that is **5–8 points**, against a target of `ρ_s ≥ 0.5` with a bootstrap CI excluding 0 — a test that is close to unpowered by construction, so a null result would be uninformative rather than evidential. **Change:** `ρ_s` is computed **within each encoder seed** over that seed's eligible readouts, giving one statistic per seed (12 per cell), and the reported quantity is the **mean per-seed `ρ_s` with a bootstrap over the seed axis** — the same paired seed axis §7 already fixes for every other statistic in the study. The **hypothesis, the separate Holm family, and the effect-size target (`ρ_s ≥ 0.5`, CI excluding 0; CI-excludes-0 below 0.5 remains *detected but immaterial*) are all unchanged from A11(d)**; only the unit of aggregation changes, from one pooled statistic to a seed-resampled mean. The pooled-over-readouts version is co-reported as a sensitivity. This is filed before any `ρ_shared` exists, so it cannot be chosen to favour an outcome; and it is a change that makes a **refutation** more informative as readily as a confirmation.

**(b) Closure fraction κ(F) = 1 − D(F, top)/D(F, linear) (additive reporting).** The training-induced deficit read across the ladder: κ ≈ 1 means a deficit present at the linear rung **closes** with probe capacity (the information was made inaccessible, not removed); κ ≤ 0 means it does not close (destroyed). κ is **defined only where a deficit exists at the linear rung to close** — exactly where the A10(c) boolean reads `suppressed` — because off a near-zero denominator the ratio is numerically explosive and substantively empty; elsewhere it is reported as `undefined`. This is the study's own absent-versus-inaccessible axis made quantitative on the same footing everywhere, and it is what lets A5's and A7's excluded arms be reported as a **measured dissociation on real data** (Scale's anchor deficit does not close, Orientation's largely does) rather than as attrition. No decision rule keys on κ.

**(c) Ground-truth destruction control (validates A12's certificate).** Greyscaling the input removes hue from the **image**, so every hue factor is absent **by construction, independently of any encoder**. `m_total` on the hue factors must collapse under `--grayscale`. This is the only case in the study where the right answer is known without measurement, and it validates the destruction certificate on real data rather than on a synthetic fixture. A certificate that does not fire here is broken and is reported as broken. Diagnostic: it enters no confirmatory family.

**(d) External-encoder check (bounded external validity).** The study's encoders are small and trained at 64×64, which is the standard scale objection. The **same** counterfactual pairs are additionally run through an ImageNet-pretrained public backbone, keeping the exact-pair property the factorial grid supplies while changing the encoder entirely. Diagnostic only: its augmentation recipe is not this study's and is not controlled, so it enters **no** confirmatory family and licenses no claim about augmentation-induced invariance — it tests only whether the displacement geometry is an artifact of this study's own training pipeline.

**Scope.** H1–H4, `S`, `ε_G`, `ε_D`, `G̃`, the A6(a) gate, the A3 exclusion, the A10(c) boolean, A12's corrected certificate and the A7(d) ratchet stop are all **unchanged**. (b), (c) and (d) are additive diagnostics; (a) changes the aggregation of one preregistered test whose hypothesis, family and target are untouched.
